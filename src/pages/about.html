About Me

My name is Kevin Potschien, and I am a strength coach for cyclists. I help riders build the strength and endurance they need to perform at their best on the bike.

Based on years of experience as a strength coach, I helped a lot of clients achieve their goals in the gym. Finding my niche in targeted strength training for cyclists brings together two of my passions in a way that's very dear to me.

My approach looks beyond the numbers: Good training has to fit the athlete: their goals and their schedule. I'm a licensed fitness coach and an amateur gravel racer with the Q36.5 Racing Experience Germany team.
